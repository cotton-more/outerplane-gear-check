# Reading the verdict

| Verdict | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Keep** | the piece is needed: wear it and upgrade it — what exactly is in the "Upgrading" block ([more](Upgrading)). "Worth reforging" — a good roll, put Reforge here first; "Top roll" — a rare roll on useful stats |
| **Stopgap** | wear it until you find better: a weapon or accessory without the needed passive, or Epic armor with only one key stat (the verdict names the missing one) |
| **Fodder** | keep it for Breakthrough: Legendary armor with weak substats, or an item with the needed passive but the wrong main stat |
| **Maybe** | your call: the details say what's in doubt (for example, the piece is good for a character outside your roster) |
| **Dismantle** | doesn't suit your characters, or the roll is weak |

## Priority chain

The order of substats a character needs, left is more important: `ATK% › CHC › SPD › CHD › DMG UP%`.

- **green** — the stat is on the piece and counts;
- **yellow** — counts for ½;
- **grey** — on the piece, but too far down the chain;
- **dashed** — not on the piece;
- **marked main** — the piece's main stat: it is never a substat too, so it isn't part of the substat score, but
  the piece has it. The main stat grows with Enhance, not Reforge, and gives more than the same stat as a substat;
- **struck through at the end** — on the piece, the character doesn't need it.

The first four places count. Two stats with a slash — for example `ATK/ATK%` — share one place in the chain and both
count: when a build lists plain "ATK", flat and % both work, and two attack substats are better than one.

## Who it suits

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cotton-more/outerplane-gear-check/main/screenshots/en/4-who-fits.png" width="360" alt="Who it suits: characters' priority chains">

First come those whose build is based on the set, then, after a divider, those who have it only in an alternative
combo: an Attack Set helmet is first of all for attackers with Attack ×4, not for Heatwave Cop Delta, who has Attack
only in 2 + 2 combos. A character with different chains in different builds shows both.

## Roll

"N of M useful, X of 3·M yellow segments on them" — and how many orange segments Reforge adds to useful stats on
average. How it's counted — [How verdicts work](How-verdicts-work#roll).
