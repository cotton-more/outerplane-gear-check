# Reading the verdict

| Verdict | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Keep** | the piece is needed: wear it and upgrade it — what exactly is in the "Upgrading" block ([more](Upgrading)) |
| **Stopgap** | wear it until you find better. A weapon or accessory without the needed passive: wear it until a recommended Legendary drops — the verdict names which. That's why an Epic weapon or accessory never goes above "Stopgap", even with a perfect roll. Epic armor — only one key stat (the verdict names the missing one) |
| **Fodder** | keep it for Breakthrough: Legendary armor with weak substats, an item with the needed passive but the wrong main stat, material for the same piece a character has below T4 (and if it beats that one — "Keep": wear it), or a Legendary that won't improve anyone: it gives nothing to anyone it suits ([Equipment](Equipment)) |
| **Maybe** | your call: the details say what's in doubt (for example, the piece is good for a character outside your roster) |
| **Dismantle** | doesn't suit your characters, the roll is weak, or it gives nothing to anyone it suits ([Equipment](Equipment)) |

## Priority chain

The order of substats a character needs, left is more important: `ATK% › CHC › SPD › CHD › DMG UP%`.

- **green** — the stat is on the piece and counts;
- **yellow** — counts for ½: 4th place in the chain, or a flat stat that gives the character less than the % one;
- **grey** — on the piece, but too far down the chain, or a flat stat that gives the character almost nothing;
- **dashed** — not on the piece;
- **marked main** — the parameter is in the piece's main (on armor and weapons also in the fixed lines: a helmet's
  HP%, a weapon's flat ATK). The main isn't part of the substat score. Flat and % are different stats of one
  parameter: when the other stat of the pair can still roll as a substat — flat ATK with an ATK% main, EFF% with a
  flat EFF — it sits next to it after a slash, dashed if the piece doesn't have it: `main ATK / ATK%` on a weapon with
  a DEF% main means every weapon has flat ATK and it doesn't count — only an ATK% substat would. The main grows with
  Enhance, not Reforge, and gives more than a substat;
- **struck through at the end** — on the piece, the character doesn't need it.

The first four places count. Two stats with a slash — for example `ATK/ATK%` — share one place in the chain and both
count: when a build lists plain "ATK", flat and % both work, and two attack substats are better than one. % counts
in full, flat by its value for the character, never above % (see [How verdicts work](How-verdicts-work)).

## Who it suits

First come those whose build is based on the set, then, after a divider, those who have it only in an alternative
combo: an Attack Set helmet is first of all for attackers with Attack ×4, not for Heatwave Cop Delta, who has Attack
only in 2 + 2 combos. A character with different chains in different builds shows both.
