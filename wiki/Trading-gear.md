# Trading gear

The app works out how to re-dress a hero (or a team of four) from the pieces already in your roster and inventory. In
the game you do it yourself — the app only calculates and tells you what to look for.

## How to open

☰ → "Trade gear"; "Trade ▸" on a hero card (straight to that hero's plan); the "Trade gear" button in the roster row
on "Characters". Only roster heroes that have builds take part.

## Hero and team

- **Hero** — pick whom to re-dress. They get the best of what other heroes and the inventory have.
- **Team** — four places in a diamond, like in the game. Members swap pieces among themselves and take from the rest.
  Press "Calculate"; change a member — "Calculate" again.
- A team member is a tile like in the character list; the pin sits in the corner instead of the star. Tap the tile to
  put another hero there or "Remove from team".
- Under the tile — the **yardstick**: the build they are dressed for ("Name ▾") or "by stats". Tap it to change.

## Keep worn gear

The pin on the hero card and tile: others do not take what the hero wears. The hero's spares (not worn) can be taken.
Members of one team can still swap among themselves. The pin is saved with the gear and is part of the backup code.

## The plan

For each receiver:

- **the gain** — "▲ +13%" in build points; if nothing is marked as worn — "▲ +41 pts"; sets that turn on or off are
  named ("Speed ×4 turns on");
- **what to put on**, by slot; under each piece, small — where it comes from ("Caren", "inventory · Caren's spare",
  "inventory") and how to find it in the game; weapons and accessories also get a substat for Secondary ↓;
- **"Skip"** — the app offers another piece;
- **an empty slot** — "Search:" with a hint of what to look for;
- **"Keep worn gear after the trade"** — for the receiver, on by default.

The plan shows only whom you re-dress: the hero or the team members; heroes you take pieces from are not shown. **Holes are not filled:** a piece taken off stays off until you enter a new one
through "Rate".

## Done, Cancel, Undo

- **Done** — all done in the game: the plan is saved for every affected hero. Right after it — "Undo".
- **Cancel** — nothing was done in the game: everything stays as it was.
- A copy that is in no pool after the trade leaves the app (it stays in your game inventory).
