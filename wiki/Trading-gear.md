# Trading gear

The app works out how to re-dress a hero (or a team of four) from the pieces already in your roster and inventory. In
the game you do it yourself — the app only calculates and tells you what to look for.

## How to open

"Trade ▸" on a hero card — straight to that hero's plan. "Trade" above the character list, and "Team trade" in
More on a phone — straight to team mode. Only roster heroes that have builds take part.

## Hero and team

- **Hero** — pick whom to re-dress. They get the best of what other heroes and the inventory have.
- **Team** — four places in a diamond, like in the game. Members swap pieces among themselves and take from the rest.
  Press "Calculate"; change a member — "Calculate" again.
- A team member is a tile like in the character list. Tap the tile to put another hero there or "Remove from team".
- Under the tile — the hero's **order** ("Order: … ▾"). Tap it to change.

## The order

What the app builds the hero for:

- **By stats** — the default: all the hero's stats and all the sets from their builds together, like the evaluation;
- **a set from their builds** — points of stats plus only that set's halves; other sets count as incidental. Armor of
  another set can fill a slot if there's no piece of the needed set;
- **pinned** — a hero with a [pinned set](Equipment#pin-a-set) has one order: "Pinned: …", strictly.

The app doesn't switch to another order itself: you choose and look again.

## The session

While the trade window is open, **what already re-dressed heroes wear** is not taken by the next ones; their spares
can be. Close the window — no restrictions remain. Members of one team still swap among themselves.

## The plan

For each receiver:

- **the gain** — how many points stronger the hero gets ("+4.2 pts"), or weaker ("Stats drop by N pts"); sets that
  turn on or off are named ("Speed ×4 activates");
- an honest line when the order can't be met: "Immunity ×2 won't come together: no Immunity gloves", or, when the
  pieces are there but below T4, "Speed ×2 won't come together: needs T4";
- **what to put on**, by slot; under each piece, small — how many points it gives over what is worn in that slot ("+1.6
  pts"; sets don't count here, they are in the hero's line), where it comes from ("on Caren", "in inventory · Caren's
  stock", "in inventory") and how to find it in the game; weapons and accessories also get a substat for Secondary ↓. The
  plan takes a recommended weapon or accessory even with fewer points — for its passive; such a piece shows "−4.4 pts ·
  better passive"; if you don't want it — "Don't take";
- **"Don't take"** — the app offers another piece;
- **an empty slot** — "Search:" with a hint of what to look for.

The pieces taken as candidates are good ones for the receiver; what they wear themselves is a candidate always. The
plan shows only whom you re-dress: the hero or the team members; heroes you take pieces from are not shown. **Holes are
not filled:** a piece taken off stays off until you enter a new one through "Rate".

## Done, Cancel, Undo

- **Done** — all done in the game: the plan is saved for every affected hero. Right after it — "Undo".
- **Cancel** — nothing was done in the game: everything stays as it was.
- A copy that is in no pool after the trade leaves the app (it stays in your game inventory).
